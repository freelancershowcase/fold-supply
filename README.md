# Fold Supply

**A shop where the room is the website.** A self-initiated brand and e-commerce concept.

> Fold Supply is a fictional brand. Nothing is for sale, no payment is taken and nothing is sent. Product photos are AI-generated concept images. The 3D products are modelled to the sizes in the brief.

## What it shows
- **E-commerce UX:** every page lives on an object in the room. The bottom bar names every section in plain words. Sizes come before prices. The Desk Set is offered when it saves money. A wrong postcode at checkout gets a clear, recoverable error.
- **Brand identity:** one move, the fold. The folded full stop is the mark. Every colour is a material. Chillax for names, Gentium Book Plus for reading and prices.
- **Immersive 3D:** live three.js products composited into a still image of a room, with a camera solved from the image. Scrolling plays a working day from 08:55 to 18:00.

## Try this
1. Scroll the desk from morning to evening, or press **Play the day**.
2. Open the **Stand** and drag the ruler from 22 to 110 mm.
3. Open the **Mat** and roll it.
4. Add all three products, open the box, and take the Desk Set swap.
5. At checkout, enter `LS1` as the postcode, then fix it.

## Source
